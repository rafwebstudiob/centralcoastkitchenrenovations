Renovating a kitchen in Avoca Beach

Avoca Beach is a beach village with older houses and newer homes on hilly streets.

Older homes in Avoca Beach often have small, closed kitchens at the back of the house. Opening them up can mean structural work, and old wiring or asbestos-containing materials may need attention.

Steep streets can limit parking, deliveries and skip bins, so mention access on the form.

Close to the coast, salt air can be hard on hardware and appliances, so ask about suitable materials.
